## Current recommendation

Test **a guided first month at the gym for women who are new or returning to strength training**. Teach the essentials as they become useful, help participants follow one coherent plan in their actual gym, and offer clearly bounded access to the trainer.

The strongest recurring need in this research is reducing uncertainty while taking action. Free programs, lessons, and AI workout apps already exist. The unproven business opportunity is delivering a better transition from confused beginner to confident participant, with support people will pay for.

The original `Gym Foundations — Business Plan.pdf` is retained as background. The newer research refines the proposed product; it does not establish market size or validate revenue projections.
