# Business decision records

This repository uses **Business Decision Records (BDRs)**: the same lightweight decision-log approach as an Architecture Decision Record, applied to products, customers, distribution, pricing, and business arrangements. The name is a repository convention, not a mandatory business standard.

A business case argues for an investment; a BDR preserves what was recommended or decided, the alternatives, the evidence, the tradeoffs, and the conditions for reconsidering it. A BDR describing founder terms is not a substitute for an executed agreement.

## Recording decisions

Include status, date, decision-makers, context, decision, justification, alternatives, consequences, and review triggers. Link supporting research and name assumptions explicitly. Use Proposed, Accepted, Rejected, or Superseded. Record who accepted a decision and when; an assistant writing a recommendation does not establish either collaborator's acceptance.

When a material decision changes, create a new record and link the superseded one. Keep the original reasoning available. Correct factual errors transparently without pretending they were the basis of an earlier decision.
