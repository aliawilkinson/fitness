# U.S. fitness market gap research

This analysis compares measured needs across fitness and supporting nutrition/recovery for a small U.S. digital business. It separates participation shortfalls, desired outcomes, unresolved service needs, and paid commercial demand.

- [Read the report](report.md): findings, cross-market comparison, competition, recommendation, and numbered sources.
- [Primary research plan](primary-research-plan.md): neutral questionnaire, sampling, definitions, and real-purchase validation. Prepared, not fielded.
- [Sources](sources.json): publisher links, periods, methods, and limitations.
- [Measurements](measurements.json): sourced observations, denominators, units, and available confidence intervals.
- [Calculated results](calculated-results.json): descriptive arithmetic; unknown commercial demand stays null.
- [Calculation script](calculate.py): standard-library Python, with arithmetic and data checks.

Run from this directory with Python 3:

```sh
python3 calculate.py
```

This extends the [earlier Reddit research](../2026-09-13-reddit-demand.md). The evidence supports testing assisted strength adoption and comparing it with mobility. It does not establish a statistically measured winner for unmet paid demand across the entire fitness market.
