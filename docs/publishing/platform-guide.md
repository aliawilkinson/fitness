# Shared platform reference

Use Product Factory's [Publishing, distribution and monetization guide](https://github.com/aliawilkinson/product-factory/blob/b1559f19afeac066110880b01906c8d2427acf55/docs/guides/publication-publishing.md). This link pins version **1.0.0** at commit `b1559f19afeac066110880b01906c8d2427acf55`, researched **2026-10-07 UTC** and adopted for this guide on that date.

The canonical guide covers Amazon KDP, eligible wider e-book/print distribution, direct sales, serial/newsletter publishing, traditional publishing and audio. It links official sources for setup, formats, pricing/royalties, identifiers, AI/content eligibility and distribution/exclusivity. Fee tables and platform rules remain there so this repository does not maintain a divergent copy.

Before **C3**, follow those primary-source links and verify current rules, title-specific eligibility, account applicability and actual quotes for the chosen route. A prior guide check is not platform acceptance or a binding price. Recheck after policy notices and before a new format, territory, distributor or exclusivity renewal. Record the check date, sources, decisions and material changes in the local milestone report; update this immutable pin through review when adopting a newer canonical guide.

Return to the [project-specific publishing guide](README.md) for the proposed offer, next milestone, project gaps and launch decision worksheet. Platform guidance does not establish Product identity, manuscript rights, medical/editorial approval or authorization to publish.
