# Publishing and earning from Personal Fitness OS research and course work

Status: proposed route, prepared 2026-10-06. The current repository contains research, curriculum/plans and reproducible descriptive calculations. The fitness application is specified, not built. This guide proposes a publication/course experiment and does not approve a paid service, clinical content, trainer agreement or software deployment. Read the [dated platform guide](platform-guide.md) for sourced channel rules and setup questions.

## Audience and first offer

The [canonical plan](../../plans/personal-fitness-os-product-plan.md) and [README](../../README.md) prioritize testing help for women starting or returning to strength training, within a broader practical fitness education idea. The [foundations curriculum](../../plans/eat-sleep-move-foundations-course.md) and [outing planner](../../plans/workout-outing-planner.md) are existing preparation assets. They are not a completed, professionally reviewed course or evidence of paying demand.

**Recommended first experiment:** finish a small trainer-reviewed lesson and companion worksheet drawn from the existing curriculum, then test whether the workflow helps the intended audience complete a concrete planning task. Use the existing [validation Project](../projects/personal-fitness-os-validation.md) to define the question and evidence before recruitment. Trainer participation and permitted use of the trainer's name/materials must be agreed before presenting this as a joint offer. A paid pilot follows those decisions and review, not merely the existence of this guide.

A useful initial paid offer could be a bounded short course/workbook with clearly defined feedback. Keep the service promise small enough to measure delivery and support time. The proposed app can be reconsidered after repeated workflow and purchase evidence; book/course publication does not require implementing it.

## Routes and revenue options

| Route | Potential offer | Why consider it | Scope to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direct digital sale | Reviewed lesson/workbook/planner bundle | A small product can test usefulness and willingness to pay | Finished content, access/delivery, support/refunds and update promise |
| Hosted course platform | A short structured course with defined lessons | Can organize a repeatable learning experience | Current platform fit/cost, video/material production, learner access and participant-data responsibilities |
| Facilitated pilot or workshop | A finite program with bounded trainer feedback | Tests the human support part of the hypothesis | Qualified trainer participation, session limits, review/safety scope, participant privacy and capacity |
| Amazon KDP | A standalone educational book or substantive workbook | A possible later format for a coherent, validated body of material | Complete reader-ready manuscript, print/e-book usability and applicable content classification rules |
| Professional licensing | Approved materials for an agreed trainer/organization use | Potential reuse if content and buyer demand support it | Ownership, adaptation/use rights, training/support and commercial agreement |

A course, coaching service and app subscription have different delivery obligations. Price and test them separately. A recurring membership needs evidence for ongoing value and a sustainable content/support cadence. Referrals or partnerships require explicit eligibility and responsibilities; they are not established revenue streams here.

## Ownership and economics before selling

[The current services proposal](../../plans/website-revenue-share-proposal.md) recommends that the trainer keep her business while the engineer supplies agreed technical services. Its rates, term and effort limits remain negotiation proposals, not an accepted agreement. This guide preserves that status and adds no new commercial commitment.

Before a pilot, record who owns content, brand, customer relationship, domain/accounts and commissioned technical work; who approves health-related material; who handles support/refunds; and how access survives a handoff. Agree which offer each sale belongs to before calculating any revenue share. Keep personal participant data out of this Git repository and MetadataDB.

For each offer, model actual receipts after platform/payment deductions, refunds and variable delivery cost. Count trainer preparation/feedback time and engineering support. Apply any revenue-share formula only after its parties agree. Separate fixed setup/content-production cost from per-participant contribution. Define maximum cohort capacity and a stop/revise threshold before accepting paid participants.

Before selecting a distributor, retain honest provenance for text, translations, cover and illustrations, and check the current topic, expertise, AI disclosure and low-content eligibility rules in the platform guide. A proposed route is not confirmed platform acceptance. Record actual author/contributor expertise where relevant; do not infer it from AI-assisted production or a completed export.

## Next milestone: reviewable pilot product

1. **Choose one learning task (C0).** Product owner and proposed trainer select the audience, lesson/workbook scope and observable learning/usefulness question from the existing plan. Record budget, capacity, owner-confirmed timeline and the evidence needed to proceed.
2. **Agree participation and boundaries (C0/C1).** Confirm trainer role/credentials as relevant, content responsibility, permissions, commercial terms, support scope and participant-data handling. Unaccepted proposal text is not a contract or permission to use a brand.
3. **Finish the selected materials (C2).** Draft and review the actual lesson, worksheet, instructions and any recording/transcript. Use the plan's professional/content review gate. The research calculator's passing assertions establish reproducibility of saved descriptive data, not health efficacy or course readiness.
4. **Prepare delivery choices (C1/C2).** Compare a direct bundle, an existing course service and a facilitated pilot using the platform guide. Record whether feedback is included, access duration, downloads, accessibility, support/refund terms and continuity if a platform fails. Prefer a bounded existing-tool experiment before a custom app build.
5. **Create the review packet (C2/C3).** Produce an approved sample, finished lesson/workbook files, truthful description, audience/scope statement, facilitator information where agreed, support process and versioned asset manifest. Preview materials as a learner and test the authorized access/delivery path.
6. **Approve the actual offer (C3).** Complete professional review, rights/account/data safeguards, economic worksheet and applicable recovery rehearsal. Record the owner's specific launch/participant-use decision. Account signup, payments, outreach and pilot delivery are later separately authorized work.
7. **Record delivery and outcomes (C4/C5).** After real delivery, retain the content version, approved aggregate findings, actual receipts/support effort and review decisions, with protected evidence locators where needed. Use the validation Project to choose continue/revise/stop and decide whether app implementation is justified.

## Launch and maintenance

The first packet needs completed learning materials, a useful sample, clear offer description, delivery/access instructions, support/refund policy, approved contributor/brand information and asset/version manifest. A curriculum outline alone is not a saleable completed course. KDP becomes a separate format-production decision when the material works as a complete book/workbook.

Maintain a versioned record of lesson/rule changes and reviewer approval. Keep learner data within the chosen protected delivery system; document permitted retention/export/deletion and who handles requests before collecting it. Reassess service capacity, costs, content freshness and platform terms between cohorts. Preserve corrected editions and communicate applicable changes through an approved channel rather than silently replacing what was sold.
